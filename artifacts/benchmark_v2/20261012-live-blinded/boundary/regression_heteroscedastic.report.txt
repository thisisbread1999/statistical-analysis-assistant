A total of 60 records were analysed. The estimated slope is 0.609 (change in y per one unit of x) with a 95% confidence interval ranging from -0.196 to 1.415, t(58) = 1.514, p = 0.1355. At the 0.05 significance level, we do not reject H0 (slope = 0). R-squared = 0.038 indicates that 0.038 of the variation in y is explained by x. The slope describes an association, not the effect of changing x.

Limitations:
The slope describes the linear association within the observed range of the predictor and should not be extrapolated beyond it.
Random allocation was not established. This analysis describes an association and does not establish causation.
Inference is conditional on the stated independent observations and the method's distribution and model assumptions; descriptive checks do not prove these assumptions.
Potential influential observations were flagged. The fitted slope may be sensitive to particular records; flagged records were not automatically removed.
The heteroscedasticity diagnostic flagged unequal error variance. Conventional ordinary least squares standard errors, p-values and confidence intervals may be unreliable; no robust covariance correction was applied.
